Hidden Machinery

What happens inside the systems you use every day.

Hidden Machinery is a small independent studio making animated explainers about computer science for curious teenagers and adults. Each episode follows one concrete event — a million people clicking Buy at once, a deleted file that can still be recovered — and opens the machine to show the mechanisms behind it. The systems in our films are made up; the mechanisms are real, and every episode lists its sources.

Our first series is Inside the Machine. Episode one: A Million People Click Buy. What Breaks First?

Our videos are published to our own YouTube channel by Hidden Machinery Studio, a private tool that runs only on our own computer.
